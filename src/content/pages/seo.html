Why local is a different game

Local search is a fight you can win.

Competing for “web design” nationally is a content marketing war against companies with budgets. Competing for “web designer near Edmonds” is a different contest, and a much shorter one.

  • Proximity beats budget

    Google weighs how close you are to the person searching. That is an advantage no out-of-town agency can outspend.

  • The map pack is the real estate

    Three local results sit above the ordinary links. Getting into them is Google Business Profile work, not blogging.

  • The intent is already there

    Someone searching “near me” has stopped researching. The page only has to make calling you the obvious next step.

  • One page per question

    Separate pages for separate searches. A single scrolling page gives Google just one answer for every query.

Structured for search

What Google actually sees.

A search engine does not see your homepage the way you do. It reads the title, the headings, the links between pages and the structured data underneath, then decides what your business is and where it works.

All of that gets written deliberately rather than left to whatever a page builder emitted. It is the difference between hoping to be found and being legible.

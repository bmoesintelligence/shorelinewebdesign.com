What the price covers

All in, itemised.

"Everything included" is an easy thing to say, so here is the actual list. The monthly plan covers all of it for the same $150, whether it is month one or month thirty.

Included

  • Custom design, built for your business
  • Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Hosting on a global CDN
  • SSL certificate, renewed automatically
  • Domain setup and DNS
  • Edits, sent over in plain English
  • Backups and ongoing updates
  • Core Web Vitals performance targets
  • WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility
  • SEO foundations and Search Console
  • Analytics on the actions that matter

Not included

  • Writing your copy from a blank page
  • Professional photography
  • Paid advertising spend
  • Third-party subscriptions (booking, online store)

None of these are upsells waiting to happen. If you need one, I'll tell you what it costs and who does it well, including the times when that isn't me.

If you cancel

You're never locked in.

No contract, no notice period, no cancellation fee. Tell me you're done and the billing stops. Here is exactly what happens to everything you have.

  • Your domain stays yours

    It's registered in your name, not mine. You can point it somewhere else the same day.

  • Your content stays yours

    Your copy, your images and your logo belong to you. Ask and I'll send the lot over.

  • The built site goes offline

    The monthly fee is what pays for the build and the hosting, so when it stops, the site stops being hosted. Nothing is held over you. It just isn't running any more.

If keeping the site whatever happens matters to you, that is exactly what the one-time build is for.

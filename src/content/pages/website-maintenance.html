Care standards · every site, every month

A website isn't finished the day it launches. Every plan covers the unglamorous work that keeps it fast, safe and current, so you never have to think about it.

  • <24h Edits

  • 24/7 Checks

Hosting, SSL, backups and content edits are all included in the monthly plan. No per-change invoices.

Why it isn't a support ticket

Maintenance is a habit, not a helpdesk.

Most care plans are a phone number you call once something is already broken. The work that actually keeps a site healthy happens quietly, every month, before you would ever notice.

  • Nothing to log into

    No dashboard, no password to reset, no admin panel going stale between visits.

  • One person who knows your site

    You are not re-explaining your business to whoever picks up the ticket. I built it, so I know what changed and why.

  • Small changes, same day

    A new price, seasonal hours, a photo swap. Send it in a sentence and it is done.

  • The dull work, handled

    Certificates, dependencies, broken links and backups. None of it is interesting, and all of it is why nothing breaks.

No tickets, no scheduled downtime

There is no queue to wait in and no maintenance window to plan around. This is what actually happens across a month, most of which you will never see.
